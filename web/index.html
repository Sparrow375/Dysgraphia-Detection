Stage 1: Adaptive Illumination & Binarization

Handwriting photographs captured with smartphone cameras frequently contain non-uniform lighting, paper shadows, camera glare, and notebook ruled lines.

How It Works:

  • Converts RGB to grayscale intensity $I(x,y)$.
  • Applies local adaptive Gaussian thresholding: $T(x,y) = \mu_{15\times15} - C$ to isolate dark ink strokes from notebook lines.
  • Falls back to Otsu's global bimodal variance maximization on uniform scans.

Stage 2: Medial Axis Skeletonization & Distance Transform

Isolates the central topological trajectory of the handwriting motion while accurately capturing pen down-force thickness variations.

How It Works:

  • Applies the Zhang-Suen thinning algorithm to iteratively erode boundary pixels while strictly preserving 8-connectivity and Euler topology.
  • Computes the exact Euclidean Distance Transform (EDT): measures the minimum Euclidean distance from each skeleton pixel to the nearest background pixel.
  • Local stroke width is calculated as $W(s) = 2 \cdot \text{EDT}(s)$.

Biomarkers Extracted:

bhk_stroke_width_variance (Thickness CoV) Optical Stylus Pressure Proxy

Stage 3: Spatial BHK Layout & Character Decomposition

Measures physical handwriting layout consistency according to the Concise Assessment Scale for Children's Handwriting (BHK).

How It Works:

  • Connected component labeling identifies discrete character glyphs $[x_{\min}, y_{\min}, x_{\max}, y_{\max}]$.
  • Calculates median character height ($H_{\text{med}}$) and width ($W_{\text{med}}$) to normalize all metrics against camera distance.
  • Clusters glyphs into text lines via vertical projection profiles and fits linear baselines $y = \beta_0 + \beta_1 x$.
  • Evaluates horizontal inter-character gaps for Shannon spacing entropy.

Biomarkers Extracted:

bhk_size_covariance bhk_height_ratio_consistency bhk_baseline_drift bhk_spacing_entropy bhk_telescoping_overlap bhk_left_margin_drift bhk_line_collisions

Stage 4: Topological Graph Traversal & Stroke Assembly

Resolves the 1,861 micro-fragment defect by reconstructing continuous physical pen strokes across intersection cliques and crossing loops.

How It Works:

  • Junction Clique Contraction: Contracts adjacent degree $\ge 3$ junction pixels into single node centroids, eliminating dead-end cycles.
  • Tangent Fly-Through Continuation: Traces crossing paths by following the branch with smallest directional deflection ($< 35^\circ$), mimicking the inertia of a moving pen.
  • Spatial Hash Stitching: Connects proximal stroke endpoints ($\le 4\text{ px}$) in $O(N)$ time.
  • Motor Prior Orientation: Reorients downstrokes top-to-bottom and crossbars left-to-right.

Biomarkers Extracted:

kin_pen_lift_count (Recovered Strokes) kin_mean_stroke_length (Arc Length) bhk_acute_turns (Direction Reversals)

Stage 5: Inverse Kinematic Modeling & Waveform Synthesis

Reconstructs the continuous speed, acceleration, and neuromuscular hesitation profile along every stroke.

How It Works:

  • Two-Thirds Power Law: Human motor control links curvature to tangential speed: $v(s) \propto \kappa(s)^{-1/3}$. The hand decelerates at sharp turns and accelerates along straights.
  • Plamondon Asymmetric Impulse Envelope: Shapes speed by $u^{0.8}(1-u)^{1.4}$ ($u = s/L$), reflecting rapid initial acceleration to peak velocity at $\sim 36\%$ duration followed by smooth glide deceleration.
  • Fluency Metrics: Quantifies Number of Velocity Inversions (NVI), Flash & Hogan dimensionless jerk ($T^5/L^2 \int j^2 dt$), and 4–8 Hz Welch tremor power.
