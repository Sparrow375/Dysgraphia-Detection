# Grade-wise Sheets (3–7): exact text for every task

One section per grade, in the order children do the tasks. Same text on paper and stylus where marked.
`|` in dictation marks where the reader pauses. All text is a **draft until a Hindi and an English teacher approve it**.

---

## Say aloud (same in every class)

**Opening**
- हिंदी: "हम अपना ऐप जाँच रहे हैं, तुम्हारी परीक्षा नहीं है। जितना अच्छा लिख सको, लिखो। मिटाना नहीं है; गलती हो तो एक लकीर खींच दो।"
- English: "We're testing our app, not you. Write as well as you can. No erasing: if you make a mistake, put one line through it."
- "When I say stop, put your pencil down and draw a small line after your last word. Don't turn the page until I say."

**Per task**

| Task | Hindi | English |
|---|---|---|
| P1 / S1 | पैटर्न को लाइन के अंत तक बनाओ। फिर बिंदु से गोल-गोल घुमाते हुए बाहर की ओर बनाओ। | Continue the patterns to the end of the line, then draw a spiral outward from the dot. |
| P2 / S2 | यह लिखावट सुंदर और साफ़ लिखो, जैसे कक्षा में लिखते हो। | Copy this neatly, the way you write in class. |
| P3 | अब पहला वाक्य तेज़ लिखो, पर पढ़ने लायक़ रहे। खत्म हो जाए तो अगली लाइन में फिर से लिखो। | Copy the first sentence as fast as you can, but still readable. If you finish, start again on the next line. |
| P4 / S3 | अब यह English वाक्य सुंदर और साफ़ लिखो। | Now copy this English text neatly. |
| P5, P6 | Dictation protocol below | |
| P7 | नीचे के सवाल का जवाब अपने शब्दों में लिखो। | Answer the question in your own words. |
| P8 | अब यही English में लिखो, अपने शब्दों में। | Now write in English, in your own words. |

**Dictation protocol:** (1) "Listen first," read the whole sentence once at normal speed. (2) Read again in chunks, about 3 seconds per chunk, while children write. (3) Read the whole sentence once more, wait 15 s, "Stop." Never spell a word, never repeat a chunk on request.

**Spelling does not matter** for free writing: "Spelling does not matter. Just write."

## Same for every grade

- **P1 / S1 graphomotor strip:** identical layout in every grade (loops row, zigzag row, 30 mm spiral with centre dot). It is the cross-grade anchor.
- **Stylus tasks (per child):** S1 (≤ 35 s), S2 Hindi copy (≤ 75 s), S3 English copy (≤ 45 s). Same sentences as above.
- **No erasers.** Cross out with one line. At time-out, draw `|` after the last word.
